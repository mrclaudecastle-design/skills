# Plain words for owners

Mila's customers are busy sole traders, often mid-job. Every word they see should be clear on a first read.

## Rules
1. **No tech words.** No "AI model", "LLM", "API", "SIP", "server", "encryption", "fine-tune", "RAG", "data pipeline". Say what it does for them instead.
   - "Your calls are answered when you can't pick up", not "AI voice agent handles inbound calls".
2. **Short.** One idea per sentence. Lead with what matters to them.
3. **Privacy is on by default.** The owner never has to set anything up to be safe. Never ask them to configure privacy.
4. **Approved privacy wording:**
   - Say "never without your OK", not "can't train" or "won't train".
   - Say "unlike most apps". Never name another company.
   - Offer simple switches: share examples (off by default), "Don't learn from this" on any call, download my data, remove my shop's data, and a recovery code.
5. **Australian English:** colour, organise, mobile, "no worries" is fine in Mila's voice but not in legal text.
6. **No promises the product can't keep,** like prices, guarantees or "100%".

## Check before you hand over
Read each line as a plumber on a ladder would. If any word needs explaining, rewrite it.
